Exercise mimetics · step 2 · LINCS L1000 signature search

Endurance exercise at 24 h, matched against 136,460 drug signatures

This report covers the MoTrPAC differential-expression result, the genes picked as the exercise signature, and the drugs whose L1000 profiles look most like it or most opposite to it. The last sections explain how to read the scores and how the pipeline was checked.

Part 1 · the input

What the DEG table contains

The input is one MoTrPAC skeletal-muscle contrast from the human pre-suspension cohort, fit with a mixed model (random effect per participant, adjusted for batch, BMI, age, site, RNA quality and sex). It is a difference-in-changes:

(Endurance 24 h post − Endurance pre) − (Control 24 h post − Control pre)

A positive logFC means the gene went up more from baseline in the endurance group than in the controls over the same 24 hours. Subtracting the controls removes changes caused by the visit, the time of day and repeated biopsies. logFC is on a log2 scale: 1 is about 2-fold, 0.5 about 1.4-fold.

Volcano plot

Raw p-value distribution

A well-behaved test gives a spike near 0 (true changes) over a flat floor (unchanged genes). Here the spike is clear and the floor flattens out from about p = 0.3. Part 2 · the query

The exercise signature

Genes with FDR < 0.05 are ranked by logFC, and the top 100 up and top 100 down are kept, choosing only among genes LINCS measures or infers. That gives eligible genes out of significant ones. The up genes have logFC ≥ and the down genes ≤ .

Without that restriction, about half of the top-ranked genes would be lost in the search, because L1000 does not measure them. They are mostly lncRNAs and pseudogenes, but they include a few muscle-metabolism genes (PLIN5, CPT1B, MYLK2, PRKAG3). genes are matched by Ensembl ID because LINCS still uses older names (for example SEPTIN7 → SEPT7, MACIR → C5orf30).

Part 3 · the method

How a drug gets its score

  1. One L1000 signature is one drug at one dose, time and cell line: a z-score for each of 10,174 genes. One exemplar per drug × cell line × dose × time is used, 136,460 in total.

  2. Enrichment. Genes in the drug signature are ranked from most up to most down. A weighted Kolmogorov–Smirnov score asks whether the exercise up genes sit near the top (es_up) and the down genes near the bottom (es_down). Each score runs from −1 to +1.

  3. WTCS = (es_up − es_down) / 2, but only if the two have opposite signs. Otherwise it is 0: the drug moves both gene sets the same way, so it gives no directional answer.

  4. NCS rescales WTCS within each cell line (positives divided by the mean positive score, negatives by the mean negative score). An NCS of 1 is an average-strength connection in that cell line.

  5. Per drug: take the strongest dose/time in each cell line, then the median NCS across cell lines. Drugs need at least 2 cell lines. n_zero counts cell lines scoring 0, and consistency is the share of the non-zero cell lines that agree on direction.

Part 5 · interpretation

How to read these results

The reverser side is the most coherent result

Half of the annotated top 50 reversers are mTOR or PI3K inhibitors (PKI-179, GDC-0980, OSI-027, PF-04691502, KU-0063794, SB-2343, taselisib), plus the IGF-1R inhibitor NVP-ADW742. Several were tested in 13–25 cell lines. Their targets fit the exercise data: MTOR itself goes up with exercise, and IGF1R goes down (see the target table). Exercise activates the IGF-1/PI3K/mTOR growth pathway in recovering muscle, so drugs that block it producing the mirror-image profile makes sense. They are good candidate blunters to follow up.

Treat the top mimickers with care

43 of the top 50 mimickers are unannotated BRD compounds. The annotated ones are broad anti-proliferative drugs: DHODH inhibitors (brequinar, SC-12267, which block pyrimidine synthesis), CDK inhibitors (AZD-5438, BMS-265246), and the transcription blockers daunorubicin and triptolide. Compounds like these score highly against many queries because they shut down growth and short-lived transcripts. Read them as "the 24 h response shares a growth-arrest component" rather than as exercise mimetics.

Statins: lipophilic ones lean reverser

Mevastatin, lovastatin, fluvastatin and cerivastatin have negative scores, while the hydrophilic pravastatin and rosuvastatin sit at 0. That fits the lipophilic-vs-hydrophilic question tested in step 3 (GSE107998). The pattern is weak: simvastatin and atorvastatin are also lipophilic and sit at 0. The myotube data is the real test.

Most controls sit at 0

Dexamethasone is a clear mimicker. Forskolin and the β-agonists score 0, although forskolin mimics exercise in about half its cell lines. At 24 h most acute cAMP/CREB response genes are gone, so a weak match is expected. Scores near 0 are fragile: swapping 4 signature genes once moved forskolin between +0.82 and 0. Metformin and statins at 0 means L1000, in mostly cancer cell lines, does not show them opposing this response. That is not evidence against blunting in muscle.

What a score can and cannot tell you

An NCS around ±1 is a typical connection; above about ±1.5 is strong. A high score in many cell lines, with few zeros and high consistency, is more trustworthy than the same score in 2 or 3 cell lines. LINCS profiles come mostly from cancer cell lines at 6–24 h, at doses unrelated to physiology. A call is a hypothesis to test in muscle, not a finding.

Part 6 · checking the work

How the pipeline was checked

PASS

Scoring matches the reference method. An independent re-implementation of the original full running-sum scoring (cmapR-style) on 3,311 real signatures gave the same es_up, es_down and WTCS to within 2 × 10−4 (one rank step from tied z-scores), with no sign disagreements.

PASS

Reading the 35 GB file. Matrix orientation and ID mapping were confirmed two ways: two read methods agree exactly, and strongly changed genes per signature track LINCS's own counts (r = 0.89 vs 0.10 shuffled).

PASS

Gene selection, normalisation and per-drug table. The top-100 lists were recomputed independently (no ties at the cutoff), and the NCS and per-drug table were rebuilt from the per-signature scores. Both match exactly.

PASS

Counts. 136,460 exemplar drug signatures and 10,174 genes (978 measured + 9,196 inferred).

NOTE

Fixed during review: median-0 drugs were labelled "reverser" (now "no signal"); consistency counted zeros (now non-zero cell lines only); "isoproterenol" is "isoprenaline" in LINCS; renamed genes are now matched by Ensembl ID.
